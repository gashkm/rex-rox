Our Mission

We are Team 1727 from Dulaney High School in Timonium, Maryland.

Our purpose is to provide all students with personalized education in the robotics space. To do so, we will allow coaches and mentors of local robotics organizations to schedule sessions where active Dulaney Robotics members will teach younger students.

Our program is open to both middle and elementary schools in our vicinity. Together, we can work towards making our STEM education stronger for everyone.
